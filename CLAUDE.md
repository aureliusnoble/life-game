# life-game

A mobile-first online creature/ecosystem game for a small group of friends. The project was restarted from scratch in November 2026 and is moving from design exploration into planning.

- `archive/` holds the superseded first version. Ignore it: do not read it for guidance, build on it, or run its tooling unless explicitly asked.
- `reports/` holds research reports; `research_notes/` holds the source notes behind each report.
- `design/` holds the current design proposals. Where a design doc and a report disagree, the design doc is the later thinking.
- `design/mockups/` holds visual studies published as pages. They are explorations, not game code. `watch-it-think.html` is the behaviour editor on a phone, with a toy simulation of its own.
- `design/checks/` holds small scripts that test claims made in the design docs. They are stand-ins, not the game's code; run with `node design/checks/moodsim.js [scenario]` (recipes), `node design/checks/verbaudit.js [scenario]` (whether each verb differs from its neighbour), `node design/checks/bodyaudit.js [scenario]` (whether each body part changes outcomes) `node design/checks/envaudit.js [scenario]` (whether each feature of the world changes who thrives) or `node design/checks/worldaudit.js [scenario]` (the six habitat patches, recovery after overuse, and ways of living through the year). `node design/checks/socialaudit.js [scenario]` runs direction checks on the social blocks. `node design/checks/castaudit.js [scenario]` tests whether hunters and their prey last together (`roomy` is slow and runs only when named). The balance lab, `node design/checks/balancelab.js check | search <habitat> | report | knockout <habitat>`, searches for designs that win and reports options that are always or never chosen; its results are kept in `design/checks/balance/`, and `design/plan.md` section 3 says how balance is tested.
- Balance is finalised at the end of the design process. Until then the checks are for direction and for catching broken tests, not for tuning numbers.
- `design/plan.md` is the plan: the decisions made so far, the design areas in order with what each must answer, and the build order that follows. Work goes into the main branch as reviewed changes.
