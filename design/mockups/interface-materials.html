Study · look, sound and feel

Interface materials

A choice to make: what is the interface made of? The same piece of a screen, three ways. The world behind is the same in each. Tap the tabs, the instincts and the buttons to feel how each one presses.

What to look at

  • Which one you would still like on the hundredth visit, and which would tire.
  • Whether the words can be read at arm's length. The instinct rows are the test: they are the core of the game.
  • How each sits on the world. The interface should be plainer than the place behind it.
  • How a press feels. Each has its own: a tag that shifts, a label that inks in, a card that sinks.
  • Mixing is allowed. A calm page with one or two handmade touches is a direction too.

What this is and is not

  • Three materials on one made-up piece of a screen. It is not a design for any screen, and the layout is the same in all three on purpose.
  • The pieces shown: a name tag, a hint, a title earned, tabs, two instincts with a strength, a way to add one, a small tally, and three actions with the day's five talks.
  • The interface is designed later, starting from what each moment should feel like. This only settles what it is made of.
  • Everything is type and code. Drawn icons and textures would change each of these, the scrapbook most.
