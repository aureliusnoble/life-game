Watch it think

The behaviour editor as it might sit on a phone: a live test tank on top, and under it the instincts as rows of paper slips. Tap a creature and its rows light up with what it is feeling the pull of right now. Tap any slip to change it and the creatures change at once.

Mossbacksday 2 · generation 3

    What to try

    1. Tap a creature. Its rows light up by how hard each instinct is pulling, in the same colours as the arrows on it.
    2. Send a hunter and watch Scared fill. Then change "move away" to "hold still", or its strength to weak, and send another.
    3. Turn on Night. Sight shrinks, and the dotted ring around the creature you are watching shows how far it can see.
    4. Open the Young tab. The young have their own, shorter page.
    5. Remove "only when hungry" from the first row and see them graze all day.

    What this is testing

    • Whether a row that reads "notice → act" is enough as the first layer, with strength, pace and conditions as small slips beside it.
    • Whether blended urges can be shown at all. Every game the research found settles conflicts by order; this one adds them up, so a creature can be pulled three ways at once.
    • Whether a form per tab is clear.

    What is not real

    • The tank runs a toy with about a dozen words, not the game's rules.
    • The creatures and the tank are placeholders for the Paper lagoon look.
    • There is one mood, already made. Making and naming moods is its own screen.
