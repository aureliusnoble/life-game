The river basin

Eight biomes on one map, from the high ground in the north to the sea in the south. Each is a region with many features: Lakeland has a lake and seven ponds, and three brooks join into two rivers. Tap a biome to read what living there is like, zoom in to see its features, and change the season or the tide.
