What this is testing

  • Whether one sheet of choices can make creatures that look far apart, and whether each part that matters in play can be read off the picture.
  • The drawing is the paper kit from the depth study, unchanged in kind: every part here is one entry in its tables.
  • The strip under the picture shows the same creature a centimetre long, as a cut-out, asleep, cold, young and dead. Every body has to wear every state.
  • Costs are placeholders. Numbers are set in the balance pass, after every part has been shown to matter.
  • A second form, such as a young one or a caste, is a second sheet that differs from the first. That editor is not built here.
