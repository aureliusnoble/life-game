Since yesterday

A test of one question: can you tell what happened to a species by looking, with almost no words? Five days in a toy world, each with one thing changed. The page does not say which. Look, turn things over, and guess.

What to try

  1. Press Play and watch a day go by. Crosses are left where creatures died, coloured by what killed them.
  2. Turn the tally: the same deaths, counted by what they were doing, where, when, and which of them.
  3. Compare their day with yesterday's, the thin bar under it.
  4. Look at the line. The coral area is the score. The dashed line is yesterday.
  5. Guess, then see what was changed.

What this is testing

  • The game records facts and never says why. Each instrument here shows only what was recorded.
  • Yesterday is drawn behind today, so that a number has something to be compared with.
  • One count of deaths can be cut several ways. Which cut is useful differs from day to day.

What is real and what is not

  • A small simulation written for this page runs each day: about thirty grazers at one pool, a hunter, two big harmless grazers. Everything on the phone is read back from its record. It is not the game's simulation.
  • One day here stands in for the time away. In the game a player is away for about twenty-four of the world's days.
  • It is a test of the idea. The layout, the look and the wording are not designs.
  • Left out on purpose: the pointer that lights the place to look, watching a creature think, and the web of who eats whom.
