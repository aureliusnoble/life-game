## Why hot, cold and ill fail

All three keep their meaning in specks. Cold's breath is two dots of about 1.5 pixels, seen in two walking frames of five; they read as bubbles, and the shrinking reads as young or calm. Hot's drops are 2 pixels, at one ear, and read as wet or crying; nothing about it is warm, and its sprawl is weary's. Ill has no change of outline, its spots are the size and place of pattern spots, and its ringed eye is wary's.

## The redesign it proposes

Rules for all three: every piece is two-tone, a light fill with a dark edge, so it shows on any body and any ground; nothing is under 5 pixels on screen; anything that rises drifts up the screen whatever way the creature faces.

## Still wrong, worst first

1. **Fed is invisible.** Bulge the middle third by 25%, with crescents 12% past the outline each side, and a rolling gait.
2. **Hungry and old are both pale lines.** Hungry: notch both flanks by 25%, ribs close up only, and never remove a body's own rim. Old: two notches cut from the outline, a 12° crook in the spine, 60% tempo.
3. **Fierce, weary and hot share a sprawl.** Fierce: front 120% wide, rear 80%, forelimbs ahead of the head, lunges.
4. **Tired has nothing at a distance.** The front third dips by 15% of the body's length and jerks back every 3 s.
5. **Playful and calm are ordinary when small.** Playful: a 35° bend, the rear raised on a doubled shadow, hops. Calm: limbs showing, so it is not cold.
6. **The plaster is lost on pale and thin bodies.** A dark edge all round, a fifth of the body's width, never under 5 pixels.
7. **Eggs read as spines; dead reads as an unprinted blank.** (Both are redesigned in the review of dunes, marsh, dead and eggs.)
8. **A blink must not hold sleep's arcs.**

## Pairs that are confused

- Cold, calm, gloomy, asleep and young: small and tucked.
- Hot, weary and fierce: sprawled.
- Hungry and old: score lines.
- Ill and wary: a ringed eye.
- Fed, tired, calm, playful and ordinary at 30 pixels.
- Fading and dead on pale bodies.

## On other bodies

The every-body sheet leaves out hot, ill and tired, so the owner's complaint is untested off the first body. Fading, dead and wary survive on all eight. Fed, cold and weary are lost or weak on most. The long segmented body is the worst: only asleep, dead and wary survive on it. The plaster is invisible on the cream one-eyed body.
