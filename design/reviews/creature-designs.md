# Review: creature designs and how far variety can go

15 November 2026. An independent reviewer, shown the eight varied creatures and close-ups of the plain and plated ones. It was told the owner's view, to weigh and test: that some of the varied designs are not aesthetic, well designed or cute, and that variety could be pushed further. Everything it proposes is specified, not drawn.

## Verdict

The owner is right on both counts, and for one reason: all eight are one teardrop with the same egg head, the same face, the same stub legs and the same heading. The variety is only what sits on the back. The fix is proportion, face, limbs and colour, not more add-ons. The plated one has the most add-ons and is the weakest.

Measured from the sheet: the head is only 5 to 7 points lighter than the body on every creature. The plated one's plates and head differ by 2 points, so they merge, and it sits only 8 to 10 points off the ground. Only the shelled one uses two hues, and it is the only resolved design.

## Redesigns

- **Plated:** one idea, armour. A pill, 1.5 to 1. Four bands the full width, overhanging the body by 8% a side for a scalloped outline. No thorns, horns or feelers; the tail one club. The head 35% of the length, peeking from under the front band like a visor. Body #E9A0B4, plates #6B2D4E, legs #45203A, accent #FFD166 on the club and the rim of the front band.
- **Hunter:** a forward stare and big paws. A wedge of a head, 40% of the length and 1.2 times the body's width. Eyes a quarter of the head's width, 0.6 of an eye apart, no brows; a dark mask band in their place. Body 60% of the head's width, tail 80% of the body's length. Forepaws twice the hind ones, sticking out 25%, claws as one scalloped piece. One notch for a fang. #2F3B52, #56688A, bone #E8E2D0, accent #FFB02E on the tail tip and inside the ears.
- **Long:** segments taper from 100% to 55%, alternating two papers; paddle legs sticking out 20%; the head 1.3 times the first segment; eyes set in from the rim; no nose blob. #7C6BD6, #B9AEF0, #4A3F8F, accent #FFD23F.
- **Swarm:** the head 60% of the length, a forked tail in the accent, eyes inside the outline. #5B9BE6, #A9CFF7, #2F5FA8, accent #FF7A59.
- **Smaller changes:** the plain one's slit becomes a pale saddle patch, and its ears move onto the head at ten and two o'clock. The swimmer's gills become three fronds a side in the accent. The breeder's head 28% of its length, and its frill a full ruff 1.4 times the head's width.

## What makes a creature cute from above

- The head 35 to 45% of the length (young 50%), and at least 90% of the body's width.
- Eyes 18 to 25% of the head's width, their centres 40 to 55% apart, in the front half, 8% inside the outline.
- The face tilted about 30° up toward the viewer; cheek dots 10 to 12% of the head's width.
- Limbs stick out 12 to 25% of the body's width, with round ends.
- At play size: twelve pieces, three kinds of add-on, nothing under 6% of the body's length.
- The papers span 35 points of lightness; touching parts differ by 12; the body is 25 points or 60° of hue off the ground. The accent under 8% of the area, in two places.
- One deliberate asymmetry, such as a patch over one eye.
- How it goes wrong: eyes on the rim, tints of one hue, a pile-up of add-ons, stub legs, detail smaller than a pixel, menace by frowning, holes that read as wounds.

## Where variety has not gone

Round bodies, bodies wider than long, ribbons. Curled, sitting and S-curved postures. Wings, long legs, no limbs. Other heads and eyes. Asymmetric marks and masks. Very dark and very pale. Tissue, fringe and pleats. Giants. Things carried or worn. Radial bodies. Outlines with holes cut through.

## Rules for the kit, so that players' combinations stay good

- The player picks hues. The kit sets the steps of lightness, and shifts anything too close to the ground or to a neighbouring species. One paper may be a second hue.
- Eyes rescale to the head and set themselves in from the rim. Things on the head slide off the eyes.
- The crown takes two of ears, horns and feelers. The back takes one of plates, thorns and a shell. Three kinds of add-on in all.
- The largest part becomes the signature and gets the accent.
- Repeated parts vanish below a floor of size; thin limbs thicken to 4 px.
- At the distance of a whole area, only the outline, eyes, signature and accent are drawn.
- Young and castes follow by rule: the head 25% larger, one fewer kind of add-on, 10 points paler.
- Refused: holes on the centre line over 20% of the body, more than three spikes, frowning brows.

## First five

1. The face: eyes placed by ratio; four shapes of head and four kinds of eye.
2. A palette maker: real steps of lightness, a second hue, a check against the ground.
3. Real limbs sticking out 12 to 25%: paddle, jointed, wing, none.
4. Body ratios from 1 to 1 up to 8 to 1, wider than long, and a curve of posture.
5. A budget of parts and the signature rule; judge everything at 1 cm and as black silhouettes. Redo the plated one and the hunter first.
