# Species sheet, second blind quiz

16 November 2026. A second independent reviewer (a fresh agent on Fable 5.2) took the quiz after the changes that followed the first one. This time each of the 62 pictures is a bare body (no nose, no ears) with one thing added.

## Result

60 of 62 right, against 59 of 63. Thirty answers were given with high confidence (22 before), 23 with medium and 9 with low (16 before). The reviewer would have named about 30 without the list. The two wrong answers are muscle and lungs, taken for each other.

## What was done about it

| Finding | Decision | What was done, or why not |
|---|---|---|
| The five organs are five outlines, and nothing says which organ each is | Accepted as a limit | An organ is a share of the inside, and it shows as build: a round belly, a deep chest, wide hips. The designer names them beside their sliders. In the world a player reads build, not organs |
| The dishes of an echo read as big ears | Accepted | Two rings of sound go out from each dish |
| The arcs off a scent gland read as sound | Accepted | A scent drifts off in dots; arcs are kept for sound |
| Locking jaws read as tentacles with suckers, and share nothing with jaws | Accepted | They take the dark paper of jaws and carry teeth, not dots |
| A tail that sheds reads as a rattle | Accepted | A row of holes at its root, where it tears off |
| Slime on the back reads as leaves | Accepted | A wet patch with a shine on it |
| Wings carry the same eye spots as false eyes | Accepted | Wings have eye spots only where false eyes were chosen |
| Slime on the sides reads as tube feet, on the front as gills | Not changed | Named correctly both times; the three slimes share one green, which is what gave them away, and that is how a covering should work |
| Heat pits and the nose are small | Not changed | Both pass the check of being seen at a centimetre; they are small parts |
| The gland's beads could be dew, eggs or pearls | Not changed | Dew is what they are. What they do shows when the creature gives |
| The ink nozzle is only a tube | Not changed | The blot shows while it squirts |
| Shell on the sides reads as a soft skirt | For the art pass | It is scored into plates like the other shells; a harder edge is a matter of finish |
| Two big cones in the spikes of the front read as horns | For the art pass | Noted in `design/look.md` |
| A feeler and a glow are both a knob on a stalk | Not changed | The rays tell them apart, and the reviewer named both |

The quiz is not run a third time at this stage. The check that remains is the one with a person who has not seen the designer.

## Hard to tell

- **4, 32, 36, 48, 58 (Gut, Fat, Muscle, Lungs, Brood).** Each is a different outline, so I can see that something changed. Nothing in the drawing says which organ it is. I matched them by where the bulge sits (shoulders, middle, rear), and any two could be swapped. 32 and 36 are also close to each other: both have a squared rear.
- **15 (Heat pits).** Two tiny marks beside the eyes. They would vanish at play size, and they could as well be nostrils.
- **3 (back slime).** Two small green patches. They read as leaves stuck on, not as slime, and the green is close to the ground colour.
- **5 and 61 (side and front slime).** I only got these because the same green appears three times. On their own, 5 reads as tube feet or extra legs and 61 as gills.
- **29 (Gland that gives).** Four pale beads. They could be dew, eggs, pearls or glow spots; nothing says "food to give".
- **39 (A tail that sheds).** It reads as a rattle. Nothing shows that it comes off.
- **56 (Echo).** It reads as a bigger pair of ears. Nothing shows a call going out.
- **41 (locking jaws).** It reads as two tentacles with suckers holding a ball.
- **45 (Ink).** A tube with a dark hole. The dark end is the only hint of ink; it could be any nozzle.
- **21 (side shell).** It reads as a striped skirt or flap, soft and not hard.
- **25 (Nose).** Clear once seen, but small and nearly the head's colour.

## Easily confused

- **19 and 56 (Ears, Echo).** Both are pink ears on the head. Echo needs a sign of sound leaving, such as rings going outward.
- **17 with Echo.** The curved lines off the yellow discs read as sound as easily as scent. If 17 is Echo, then 56 and 19 are both left for Ears.
- **11 and 53 (front spikes, Horns).** Both are pale points on the head aimed forward. The many small points round the eyes are what make 11 spikes; its two big cones alone would be horns.
- **57 and 38 (Sting: front, Mouth: tongue).** The list says a tongue "strikes from a distance", which is what 57 looks like. I chose sting only because it carries the same arrowhead as 51.
- **1, 43 and 22 (front shell, front bristles, Crest).** All three are a ruff at the neck. 43 and 22 differ mainly in colour (brown, yellow), and a brown crest would be 43.
- **41 and 40 (locking jaws, suckers).** White dots on red arms read as suckers in both.
- **23 and 41 (jaws, locking jaws).** They share nothing, so a player would not see them as two kinds of one thing.
- **30 and 31 (one broad foot, a skirt of cilia).** Both are a pale rim round a legless body; only the edge differs (scalloped, fine-toothed).
- **31 and 60 (skirt of cilia, side bristles).** Both are a fine fringe on the flanks. Colour and the missing legs tell them apart.
- **8, 9 and 12 (webbed feet, paddles, fins).** All are "legs turned into flat things". Fins are clear by their pink ribs; 8 looks feathery more than webbed, and 9 looks like stumps.
- **55, 18 and 44 (back bristles, mud, leaf litter).** All are a brown patch on the back. The jagged edge is the only thing marking 55 as bristles.
- **3 and 59 (back slime, grass).** Both are green marks on the back.
- **2 and 24 (False eyes, Wings).** The wings carry the same yellow eye-spots, so a player may think wings include false eyes.
- **16 and 39 (Club tail, tail that sheds).** Both are a knob on the tail. 16 also looks like a toadstool.
- **27 and 46 (Glow, Touch).** Both are a stalk with a yellow knob from the head. The rays are the only difference.
- **27 and 29 (Glow, Gland that gives).** Pale beads could be glow spots.
- **20, 50 and 40 (hooks, arms, suckers).** The same extra limb with a different tip. They are clear at this size, but the tip is small.
