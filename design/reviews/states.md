# Review: how a creature's state is shown

15 November 2026. An independent reviewer, shown the sheet of eight states, the ordinary creature, the sleeping pose in the scene, the startle film and the eight varied bodies. It also shrank the sheet to 30 px, the size of a creature seen as a 5 mm blob.

## What is wrong, worst first

1. **One silhouette.** The signals live in eyelids, 3 to 4 px on a phone. Build a posture rig that every body has: the stretch of the spine, the width at the middle, the head's offset and turn, how far the limbs spread or tuck, and the shadow's offset. Key every state there first and in the face second.
2. **Paleness carries cold, old, dead and fading, and hue belongs to the player.** Keep lightening for fading alone. Cold: limbs tucked, head in, shivers in short bursts, a puff of breath. Old: two or three creases scored across the back, a nick in an ear or fin or shell edge, head low. Dead: turned over showing a plain underside, limbs straight, no shadow offset.
3. **Hungry and fed as 6% of width cannot be seen,** and a thin build looks starving anyway. Change the middle of the body only, by 20%: hungry has notched flanks, scored ribs and a sweeping head; fed has flank crescents bulging past the outline, and sits.
4. **Hurt is camouflaged.** A plaster in a colour kept for it, which no pattern may use: white with dark stitches, a fifth of the body's width, mid-back. One limb held in, a lopsided step.
5. **Wary equals neutral.** Shrink the ordinary pupil to 70% so that "wide" has room.
6. **Startle is rigid.** Two frames: size 1.25, the spine squashed to 0.85, the shadow's offset tripled, ears and limbs snapping out 30°; it lands facing the tap.
7. **Closed eyes must mean asleep only.** The shadow is unused: make height mean energy.

Weary spreads and gloomy contracts. Gloomy has no shiver, which is cold's, and no fixed stare, which is wary's. Fierce points at its target and wary angles away.

## States not yet shown

- **Tired:** comes in episodes: the head nods and jerks back, one lid lower, long blinks.
- **Hot:** the opposite of cold: limbs splayed, the body flattened wider, a fast panting pulse, a flicked drop.
- **Ill:** a staggering path, unequal pupils, two or three ringed spots stuck on. Stung: one raised bump.
- **Carrying eggs:** a pear shape, widest in the rear third, egg ovals at the rear rim; a waddle.
- **Young:** the head 60% of the length, eyes closer, stubby limbs, a simpler pattern.
- **Fading:** the only change of palette. The pattern fades first, edges get a ragged pale rim, and every member looks alike.
- **A glance:** an ear or feeler swivels first, the pupils shift by 30% of the eye's radius, then the head; the body keeps going.

## Rules that work on any body

- **Always available:** the posture rig, heading relative to a target, tempo and pauses, a tremble, the shadow, and pieces fixed mid-back or beside the body.
- **Stand-ins:** for ears: feelers, then horns, fins, a tilt of the head. For a tail: the last segment, then hind limbs, a sway of the rear. For a tuck: fins fold, segments bunch, a shell draws in.
- **Every state gets one large channel and one close-up channel.**
- **Never:** the mouth; absolute width; hue or lightness; marks in the colours of a pattern, or under ears and plates; lids on dot eyes; letters.

## At 5 mm

Today only asleep survives by shape. These must read: dead (turned over, no shadow, still), asleep (a curl, a slow pulse), hurt (a white dot of 4 px and a limping rhythm), hungry (a zigzag search), wary against fierce (a freeze against a lunge), and fading. For needs the player must act on, add a paper token beside the creature at a constant size on screen.

## First five

1. Build the posture rig; no state keeps the ordinary silhouette.
2. Paleness for fading only; redo cold, old and dead.
3. The plaster in its own colour, and a limp; flank notches and bulges.
4. A smaller ordinary pupil; frowning lids for fierce only; closed eyes for sleep only.
5. Rebuild the startle, then test every state on all eight bodies at 30 px with the captions covered, and record what is confused.
