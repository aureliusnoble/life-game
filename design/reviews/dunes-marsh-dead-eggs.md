# Review: dunes, marsh, dead and eggs

15 November 2026. The owner asked for a hard look at these four: "we need to pay the same attention to detail we did to the lake to every biome, e.g. dunes and marshes could use some work. Also the dead state doesn't look great," and "for dead we may want skeleton or ones with meat in various states so we can see things feed on them." An independent reviewer was shown the pond scene as the standard, the dunes and marsh cells large, and the dead and egg-carrying states, and was asked to design the fixes. Positions are on a screen of 360 by 360 units. Heights: F lying flat, L low, B a body, T tall, O overhead.

## Dunes: 3 out of 10

**Wrong, worst first:** no composition, only three look-alike crescents; one value; one outline in three sizes, which reads as a hoof print; hairline ripples everywhere; the pond's green grass standing in for marram; no hollow, driftline or seeds.

**The design.** One dune to a screen. Wind and light both come from the upper left, so slip faces are always in shade.

Ripples are three long overlapping strips along the ramp's lower edge: cut, not scored.

## Marsh: 3 out of 10

**Wrong, worst first:** it is a mudflat, not a marsh, and "the best cover on the map" has four tufts; brown water on brown mud reads as tar; hairline cracks leave no quiet ground; the islands are one oval in three sizes; no mat, drowned tree or seed heads.

**The signature changes.** It is the reed stand, cut as a terrace: a dark base #55683E seen only as gaps, a body #7C9550, and a top #A8AE62 set toward the light. Its edges toward the water are a fringe of uneven blade tips 6 to 10 units long, leaning downwind, with seed heads in threes. Creatures pass under it and vanish.

## Dead: 2 out of 10

**Wrong:** it throws away the species' colours, so nobody knows whose it is; a cream blank reads as a missing picture and vanishes on sand; it is pale like fading; nothing shows feeding.

**The rule.** A creature is a stack of skin, flesh and bone, and death takes it apart from the top.

- **Skin** is its own sheets turned face down: its own colour mixed 45% with kraft #CFC3AE, without its marks or its face.
- **Flesh** #D9737E, always edged with a 2-unit rind #F4DDCB, and **bone** #EFE4CC are the same for every species. Rose showing means food is left.
- **Under the stack** lies its outline in the ground's colour made 15% darker, seen only where the body has gone, so bone reads on any ground.

|---|---|---|
| Fresh | All sheets face down, slipped 2 units out of register; the head 25° off the body's line; limbs uneven; a 1-unit shadow | A dull lopsided shape in its own hue, with no eyes |
| Opened | One scalloped bite, a quarter of the body sheet | A bitten apple |
| Part eaten | Each feed takes a scallop. Skin stays at the head and the ends of limbs; ribs show as ivory bars in the rose; odd limbs gone | Rose and ivory stripes |
| Bones | No skin or flesh; hard parts keep their dulled colour. A body nothing eats arrives here without ever showing rose | An ivory comb on a dark patch |
| Returned | The ground laps over all but two or three tips of bone; the patch grows three to five of the biome's flowers | A flower patch the shape of a body |

## Eggs: 3 out of 10

**Wrong:** the pear outline changes the species' silhouette and sits next to "fed". The eggs are 3-unit bumps in the cream of its toes; at 30 pixels they are not there. Nothing says how many or whose.

**An egg** is a blunt-ended oval cut in two arcs, low, tinted 30% with the parent's colour in #FBF3E4, with three speckles in the parent's accent, so a clutch says whose it is. Large: 16 by 12, one or two. Medium: 11 by 8, three or five. Spawn: beads of 4 units, only as a raft 28 across of nine to fifteen in pale jelly.
