# Second review: creature designs, and whether the body editor can make them

15 November 2026. An independent reviewer, shown the sixteen creatures from the kit large and small, as one-colour cut-outs, at about 30 pixels in twelve conditions, and two of them walking the scene. It was told the owner's two remarks: that some of the varied designs were not aesthetic, well designed or cute and that variety could go further; and that the body editor has to let a player be as expressive as this.

## Verdict

Half answered. The variety is real in the eight new creatures; the first eight are still one animal with things on it. Five are below the bar and none is a nine. The body editor as written in `design/body.md` reaches four of the sixteen.

## The set

- The new eight vary in plan, proportion and limbs. Colour is much better: thirteen use a second hue.
- Faces do not vary: ten share one glossy eye on the side rim of the head, five share one cream muzzle. Eleven are a bean of a head butted onto a blob. All face the same way.
- As cut-outs, the second row is distinct. In the first, the plain one, the plated one, the breeder and the giant are one lumpy shape, and the swimmer is that shape with a tail.

## Redesigns for the weakest five

- **Flit.** A furry pill of a body a third of the span. Forewings swept back 20°; hindwings 60% their size, half tucked under: a moth, not a propeller. One eye-spot to each forewing. Head 40% of the body with a dark ruff behind. Round eyes set in, with pale half-moon lids. Feathered feelers from the crown, clear of the eyes. Wings #FBF6EC, body #B77E9A, head #E7C6D2, ruff and feelers #6E4A5F, spots #F2A541.
- **Hopper.** A pear, hips one and a half times the shoulders. No separate head: a paler mask over the front 35%. Hind legs in three pieces folded flat to the flank, knees 30% past the hips, long three-toed feet. Small forelegs with toe pads. Dome eyes with yellow irises and bar pupils; a wide line of a mouth. #2F6FDE, #9CC0F5, legs #173A8C, irises and pads #FFD23F.
- **Bolster.** A barrel 1.3 to 1, widest at the hips. Head 22%, with pale cheek rounds 1.4 times its width. Four round feet sticking out 15%; a tufted tail. One cream patch on one hip. Two paler, unpatched young in a row along the spine. Dot eyes. #B5532F, head #C96A42, cheeks #F3E6D0, feet #6E2F1A, young #E39A6E, accent #7FD1C7 in the ears and tuft.
- **Bloom.** A bell with five scalloped lobes, pale #FFF7D1 over #FFE98A, the slots cut as petals. Five ribbon arms 1.2 times the bell's width, curved one way like a pinwheel and streaming behind when it moves: #E0A82E, bead tips #FF6B9D. Two dot eyes and cheeks; no smile.
- **Plated.** A pill 1.6 to 1. Five crescent bands the full width, overlapping like roof tiles, overhanging 10%, narrowing to 70% at the tail. The front band covers the top third of the head. A thick tail ending in a knob, with no stick. A dark dot of a nose in place of the plain one's muzzle. #E9A0B4, #6B2D4E, band edges #8E4169, #45203A, #FFD166.

## What the kit gets wrong for every creature

- **Faces.** One eye, and one muzzle or no mouth; eyes on the side rim, looking sideways. It needs sets of eyes, lids and mouths.
- **Heads.** Always a separate bean with a hard seam, often in the body's own paper.
- **Limbs.** Walking legs are still toe dots under the body; forelegs vanish under big heads. Every limb and tail is one straight stiff piece.
- **Palettes.** The accent is often a few pixels. Three bodies match the ground's lightness and separate by hue alone. There are only three patterns: stripes, spots and a patch.
- **Layering.** One thickness and one soft shadow for every piece, so it reads as stickers. No fold, cut edge or translucency.

## Bodies it cannot make yet

1. A bent body with limbs: a lizard's sprawl, a curled tail, a neck.
2. Soft curving limbs: an octopus, a jellyfish's trail.
3. A lopsided body: a crab with one big claw, a snail with its shell to one side.

## Can the body editor make these?

The biggest gap is that the editor has no head. Its shape is one outline, and every drawing here depends on a separate head and its size.

| | Creatures |
|---|---|
| Yes | Shelled, Two forms, Puffle, and Swarm if its fork is a tail fin |
| In the rules, but not in looks | Plain (head size, tail), Plated (a shell as bands, a club tail), Hunter (head shape, tail), Swimmer (gill fronds), Hopper (folded legs), Bolster (head, cheeks; riding young is the pouch) |
| No | Long (segments, which the body design defers), Ribbon (segments, a posture curve, moving without limbs), Stilt (a single eye set on top, not on the outline), Lugg (a thing worn), Bloom (arms, their count, holes, a face at the centre of a body with no front), Flit (wings) |

**The smallest additions that reach all sixteen.**

Looks only:

1. A head: its size and shape, or none.
2. A tail: its length and its tip.
3. How the body is cut: one piece, rounds with a count, holes.
4. A curve of the spine and a resting pose.
5. A face: the style and number of eyes, lids, a mouth, with parts allowed on top of the body and not only on its outline.
6. A style for each covering: a dome or bands, a fringe or a ruff, fronds.
7. A count of arms for the plan with no front.

Would have to mean something in the rules:

8. Wings: a new mover, cheapest as short bursts on land, the twin of the tail fin.
9. Moving without limbs: a body-wave mover, or the frill counted as a fin.
10. Arms, if they grip or feed; otherwise the skirt of cilia is drawn as arms.
11. A worn thing, if found things are objects in the world; looks only if it is how leaf-litter camouflage or a shell is drawn.
12. A club tail, if it hits.
