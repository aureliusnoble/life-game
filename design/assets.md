# Assets: what exists and what the world needs

Draft 1, 15 November 2026. The owner asked for this: assets outside the lake are far thinner than the lake's, the lake itself needs more than one screen's worth, and there are no general ones. The lists below are an independent reviewer's inventory, made from the habitats, foods and cycles in `design/world.md`, so that every food and every kind of cover a creature can use has something to look at. Nothing here is built unless it says so.

How an asset is made is in `design/look.md`, section 17, set 5. The review that produced this is `design/reviews/round2-assets.md`.

## 1. What exists

Twelve, on the asset sheet in `design/mockups/paper-study.html`: grass, reeds, flowers, toadstools, a log, driftwood, a stump, stones, fallen leaves, lily pads, a butterfly and a rock pool. Their mean score is 6 out of 10 and none reaches 8.

**The largest fault is of kind, not quality:** every one is a garnish of 12 to 55 units. Nothing can hide a creature, feed a herd or fill ten screens. Heath, Grassland and the Tidal flats have no study at all.

**Built since this list was made,** in the highlands and the woodland: slabs of rock with walls and strata, a tarn, a cave mouth with pale caps, a crevice, a spring, a scree fan, lichen that can be eaten, a snow drift, blown seed; a trunk, folded roots that dive under the floor, leaf drifts that are scuffed aside, a brook with a log across it and brackets of fungus, a thicket with berries, a glade, ferns, nuts, fruit on a bough and fallen fruit that can be eaten. In the dunes and marsh places and the remains: marram, a sand tail, sea holly, a mat of hollow plants, a tumble-seed, wrack, three shells, a burrow mouth; a reed stand that a body goes under, tussocks of sedge, kingcups, sprays of seed, duckweed, a drowned tree, a floating mat, a nest with eggs, a dragonfly; and bones, as the fourth stage of remains. The dunes' and the marsh's own things are now on the asset sheet too, with two general ones: eggs in every stage (a clutch in a scrape, a nest, one hatching, one emptied, one eaten), and tracks and marks (prints for four kinds of foot, a drag, a scent mark, a burrow).

## 2. Levels

| Mark | Level | Shadow |

| B | A body | 5 units |
| T | Tall | 9 units |
| O | Overhead | Far from the thing |
| R | A recess, cut down into the ground | Shade inside its lip |

**Bold** is to be built first.

## 5. What the guide needed before these could be made from it

The reviewer could not have made a nest, a burrow or snowy grass from the guide as written. All eight are now written, in `design/look.md` section 20, each taken from something built. It needed:

1. **Recesses:** how a thing cut down into the ground is shaded, how many steps, the darkest paper.
2. **Masses and cover:** stands and swards that fill a screen, and how a body inside one still shows.
3. **States of an asset:** eaten, burnt, flooded, frozen, snowed on, occupied. The same outline with pieces swapped.
4. **Traces:** prints, trails and marks, which age by losing pieces.
5. **A palette:** each ground by biome and season, and papers for wood, stone, earth, bone and snow.
6. **Solid as its own property,** apart from height: what stops a creature, what it goes under, what it goes into.
7. **Food may carry the accent.**
8. **Numbers** for the light, the shadow's tone and the steps of edges and folds, and one worked example in code.

## 6. Order of building

1. Extend the guide with section 5.
2. The general set: prints, a burrow, a nest with eggs, bones, a scent mark.
3. The three kinds the world lacks most: a stand that hides a body (reeds, tall grass), a sward that can be grazed down, and a recess.
4. The five in bold for each biome, as each biome's scene is made.
